Plant Care Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

This plant care app is built to work without an account. Your garden data is stored on your device unless you choose to send a photo or question to the plant advisor.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your plants, care logs, photos, reminder settings, and app preferences in your browser using localStorage and IndexedDB. This data stays on your device and is not stored in an app database.

Plant advisor and photos

When you use Ask, your question, any attached photo, and limited garden context such as configured plant names, plant types, and your saved location label are sent to Google Gemini so it can generate a plant-care answer. Do not upload photos that contain private documents, faces, addresses, or anything you do not want processed by Google.

Weather and location lookups

If you type a city or ZIP code, the app uses Open-Meteo to look up coordinates and weather for that place. If you choose the location button, your browser asks for permission first and the app may use OpenStreetMap Nominatim to turn device coordinates into a city label.

Analytics

If you accept analytics, the app may use PostHog to understand anonymous product usage, such as which tabs are opened and whether common actions work. Analytics are optional. If you decline, PostHog is not initialized.

Cookies and local storage

The app uses browser storage to remember your plants and your analytics consent choice. Clearing browser data will remove your garden unless you exported a backup first.

Data export and deletion

You can export your plant data from the app. To delete local data, clear this site's browser storage from your browser settings.

No sale of data

The app does not sell your personal information and does not run ads.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app maker through the project page or wherever you received the app link.
