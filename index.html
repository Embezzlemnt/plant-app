Questions people ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. Core care is free. Optional paid features may come later.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works without signup.

Where is my data stored?

Your plants and logs are stored on your device. You can export a backup.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, but iOS Safari has limited web push support. Android Chrome is best for reminders.

How does the AI work?

Ask can send questions and photos to Google Gemini. The app explains this before photo upload.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the app shell is cached after first load. AI and weather need network access.
